date, status and source year. A proposed figure is an assumption for planning and can still change before the law takes effect.

The full projection supports purchases of a main residence only. Its pension
withdrawal calculation uses one flat rate; it does not calculate progressive Box 1
tax on a large withdrawal.

## Amounts carried from an earlier year

The 2027 bracket amounts, credits and Box 3 allowances still need confirmed current
official amounts. The September tables were followed by amendments, including the
[third note of amendment](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D55208).
The plan therefore retains the earlier sourced amounts for these fields.

Each figure keeps its own source year. If tax indexing is enabled, the projection
indexes eligible amounts from that year using the plan's inflation assumption. A new
2027 entry for one field does not reset other fields' indexing. An amount you edit
is in the selected year's money and applies only to that year.

The tax panel lists the unindexed source amounts. An older amount shown there is a
planning estimate once indexed, rather than a published 2027 allowance.

The 2027 investment forfait is unresolved: the software workbook gives 6.38%,
while the later amendment explains a 1.5 percentage-point increase to 7.87%,
implying 6.37% before that increase. The plan retains the explicitly labelled
2026 rate until a consistent official figure is available. Neither value has been
guessed from rounded figures.

## Future Box 3 and AOW assumptions

The baseline retains the earlier WWR proposal from 2028 as an explicit assumption.
The government's [3 November 2026 letter](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D55209)
proposes a different tax based on realized gains, aimed at 2029. The plan does not
implement that proposal. Future tax and FI results can change substantially if its
rules take effect.

AOW ages beyond the fixed schedule through 2031 use the plan's growth assumption.
That is separate from the SVB's expected ages. Check the [SVB age overview](https://www.svb.nl/nl/aow/aow-leeftijd/uw-aow-leeftijd)
and set a person's pension age when the plan needs a different assumption.

Use the [comparison guide](community-validation.md) to check a supported tax year or
AOW case against an official source without sharing personal household data.
