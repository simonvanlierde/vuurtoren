# Which tax figures does the plan use?

Checked on 14 November 2026. The tax panel shows each figure's source, verification
date, status and source year. A proposed figure is an assumption for planning and can still change before the law takes effect.

## Check one projected tax year

Open **Plan → Tax burden → Check my tax** and choose a year. The view runs the same
deterministic plan as the chart, including scenario overrides and inflation indexing.
Each Dutch rule shows its result, the figures it used, and their source, status,
verification date and source year. These are the planner's calculations, not figures
to copy into a tax return.

Box 3 shows each taxpayer's balances, debt threshold, allowance, forfaitair income,
tegenbewijs, or the assumed WWR income and loss carry. Fiscal partners share a
calculation; other owners have separate calculations with joint balances split equally.
The assessment uses the model's year-end balances, not the statutory 1 January valuation.
A projection that starts mid-year includes only its projected months.

Box 1 shows the housing adjustment and each pension owner's marginal payout tax,
with the brackets and credits before and after the withdrawal. Ordinary income is
entered net, so this is not a complete wage-tax assessment. Investment-tax totals include
citizenship overlays, whose individual rules are outside this Dutch explanation.
Explanations currently require Dutch residence throughout the plan and a December
assessment inside the projection. A plan with a change of residence is refused.

## Tax on a pension or lijfrente withdrawal

Every pension account has an owner, and a withdrawal is Box 1 income on top of that
owner's other income that year. An account saved without one by an earlier version gets
the household's only person; otherwise the plan asks you to pick one. The plan
charges the difference it makes to the year's tax: the brackets, the first one without
the 17.90% AOW premium from AOW age (blended by month in the year AOW age is reached, as
the Belastingdienst table does), and the algemene heffingskorting, arbeidskorting and
ouderenkorting with their phase-outs. The 2026 figures come from the Belastingdienst
tables for the [brackets](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/boxen_en_tarieven/box_1/box_1),
the [algemene heffingskorting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/algemene_heffingskorting/tabel-algemene-heffingskorting-2026),
the [arbeidskorting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/arbeidskorting/tabel-arbeidskorting-2026)
and the [ouderenkorting](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/voorlopige-aanslag/content/voorlopige-aanslag-tarieven-en-heffingskortingen),
and the AOW premium from [Staatscourant 2025, 42324](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-42324.html).
In 2027 the AOW-age first-bracket rate follows the proposed 35.84% minus that premium.

The other income is what you entered as income flows, which are take-home pay, so the
plan grosses them back up through the same schedule. Below AOW age it counts as wages,
from AOW age as AOW and pension. AOW counts only when you enter it as an income flow.
An income flow without an owner is split equally between the household's people. The
kortingen phase out on Box 1 income only, not on Box 3 income, and the
alleenstaande-ouderenkorting is not included.

## Amounts carried from an earlier year

The 2027 credits (with their phase-out points and rates), Box 3 allowances and eigenwoningforfait threshold still need current official amounts.
The September tables were followed by amendments, including the
[third note of amendment](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D55208),
which raises the 2027 indexation factor from 1.01248 to 1.015548, so the September amounts are stale.
Amendment 42 also changes the arbeidskorting and ouderenkorting; a current complete credit table is still needed.
The minimum-wage-linked phase-out points also depend on the November 2026 minimum wage.
The Wet Hillen percentage is derived (2026 value minus the yearly step) and has no 2027 entry.
The plan therefore retains the earlier sourced amounts for these fields.

Each figure keeps its own source year. If tax indexing is enabled, the projection
indexes eligible amounts from that year using the plan's inflation assumption. A new
2027 entry for one field does not reset other fields' indexing. An amount you edit
applies only to the selected year. It is in the same source-year prices as the figure
it replaces, and the projection indexes it from there.

The tax panel lists the unindexed source amounts. An older amount shown there is a
planning estimate once indexed, rather than a published 2027 allowance.

The 2027 investment forfait is unresolved: the software workbook gives 6.38%,
while the later amendment explains a 1.5 percentage-point increase to 7.87%,
implying 6.37% before that increase. The plan retains the explicitly labelled
2026 rate until a consistent official figure is available. Neither value has been
guessed from rounded figures.

## Future Box 3 and AOW assumptions

The baseline retains the earlier WWR proposal from 2028 as an explicit assumption.
The government's [3 November 2026 letter](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D55209)
proposes a different tax based on realized gains, aimed at 2029. The plan does not
implement that proposal or amendment 80's progressive Box 3 rates from 2029.
Those rates are 35.5% up to €60,000 taxable income and 38.5% above.
Future tax and FI results can change substantially if these rules take effect.

AOW ages beyond the fixed schedule through 2031 use the plan's growth assumption.
That is separate from the SVB's expected ages. Check the [SVB age overview](https://www.svb.nl/nl/aow/aow-leeftijd/uw-aow-leeftijd)
and set a person's pension age when the plan needs a different assumption.

Use the [comparison guide](community-validation.md) to check a supported tax year or
AOW case against an official source without sharing personal household data.
